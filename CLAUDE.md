# CLAUDE.md — Sambrano Roofing & Exteriors LLC Website

This file establishes the project rules for any AI assistant (or human contributor) working on this website. It governs how brand identity, design, content, and code changes are handled going forward.

## 1. Brand Source of Truth

- **"Sambrano Roofing & Exteriors LLC Brand Guide v1.0-2.pdf"** (located in this project's root) is the authoritative source for the company's visual identity and website design standards.
- Do not invent or redefine brand standards.
- If the Brand Guide and an existing website implementation conflict, flag the conflict before making a major change.

## 2. Logo

- Use only the approved official Sambrano Roofing & Exteriors LLC logo.
- Preserve its original proportions and clear space.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, redesign, replace, or add effects to the logo.
- The approved white logo with transparent background is the primary logo currently selected for the website.
- Do not create a new logo variant unless explicitly approved.

## 3. Colors

- The official brand palette is Gold, Charcoal/Black, and White.
- The Brand Guide states that exact HEX/RGB/CMYK values are not yet finalized.
- Never invent, substitute, or silently redefine official brand color values.
- If an existing website color is outside the documented official palette, flag it for Erick's approval before making a major brand change.

## 4. Typography

- Inter is the standard body/UI font.
- Lexend Deca is the standard heading font.
- Do not replace these fonts without explicit approval.

## 5. Brand Voice

- Professional, honest, clear, confident but not arrogant, helpful, respectful, and locally grounded in Tucson/Southern Arizona.
- Never use exaggerated or unsupported claims.
- Do not invent licenses, certifications, warranties, insurance coverage, services, guarantees, or performance claims.
- Avoid hype, false urgency, aggressive sales language, and unnecessary jargon.

## 6. Photography

- Prefer authentic Sambrano Roofing & Exteriors project photography over stock imagery.
- Do not create misleading before/after imagery.
- Do not misrepresent projects, locations, materials, dates, or services.
- Preserve customer privacy and require appropriate consent for identifying customer imagery.

## 7. Website Design

- The website should feel modern, clean, premium-but-approachable, trustworthy, and professional.
- It must be responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Prioritize clear navigation, strong hierarchy, readability, accessibility, performance, and conversion without being pushy.
- Primary content flow should clearly communicate who the company is, services, service areas, reasons to trust the company, completed work, and how to contact the company.

## 8. Accessibility

- Maintain appropriate color contrast.
- Use meaningful alt text.
- Maintain semantic heading hierarchy.
- Keep interactive elements keyboard accessible.
- Preserve accessible modal behavior.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Animations must never be required to understand or use the website.

## 9. Animation

- Animation should be purposeful, smooth, professional, subtle, and performance-conscious.
- Avoid excessive motion, distracting effects, unnecessary animation, or animation that interferes with usability.
- Always provide reduced-motion behavior where appropriate.

## 10. Existing Site

- The current site is a plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript static site.
- Do not introduce a framework, bundler, or dependency unnecessarily.
- Preserve existing working functionality unless a change is explicitly requested.
- Before making significant architectural changes, explain the reason and obtain approval.

## 11. Content and Accuracy

- Never invent project details, customer information, locations, dates, materials, reviews, credentials, or results.
- If required information is missing, ask Erick rather than making it up.
- The contact form must not promise a response time unless that functionality is actually implemented and approved.

## 12. Governance

- Erick Sambrano is the final decision-maker for the company's brand identity and major website changes.
- Major changes include logo redesigns, new official colors, typography changes, major layout redesigns, major navigation changes, or other changes that materially alter the brand.
- Clearly identify major changes and ask for approval before implementing them.
- Minor implementation improvements may be made when they clearly follow the Brand Guide and existing approved direction.

## 13. Workflow

- Work incrementally.
- Before changing files, inspect the existing implementation and explain what will change.
- For major changes, propose the change first and wait for approval.
- When implementing an approved change, modify only the files necessary for that change.
- Do not make unrelated improvements while working on another task.
- After changes, verify that existing functionality still works.

## 14. Project-Specific Priority

When instructions conflict, use this priority:

1. Explicit instructions from Erick in the current task.
2. The Sambrano Roofing & Exteriors LLC Brand Guide.
3. CLAUDE.md project rules.
4. Existing website implementation.

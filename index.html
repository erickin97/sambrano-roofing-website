About Us

Licensed, local, and built for desert roofs.

Sambrano Roofing & Exterior LLC is owned and operated by Jose Erick Sambrano Torres, working on tile, shingle, and flat roofs across Tucson and Southern Arizona. We know how the desert heat, UV exposure, and monsoon storms wear down a roof — and we build our repairs and installs around that reality.

Every job is backed by licensed, bonded, and insured work, with free estimates on every project.
